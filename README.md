**Welcome to Blurnie - The Art Of Enabling Blur & Animation.**

This Module Enables Blur & Animations On ColorOS 11 & Above.

# Disclaimer 

> [!CAUTION]
> **I am not responsible for bricked devices, nuclear detonations, time travel, or your phone becoming a government experiment.**


>[!NOTE]
> **No Performance Guarantee**:
This module does not promise any performance improvements or optimizations on your device.
>
> **Usage Note**:
Blur and animations were disabled by Realme to keep memory usage low and prevent UI lag on limited-RAM devices. By installing this module, you are forcing the system UI to enable blur and animations. This may result in a laggy experience if your device's SoC cannot handle the additional load.

# Requirements

> Supported Platforms: Magisk / KernelSU / Apatch

> Compatibility : This module targets devices running Android 11 and Above (Based On ColorOS)
